One small word, a long journey

The history
of hello.

The familiar greeting entered English in the early 1800s, first appearing in forms such as “hallo” and “hullo.” It was used to call for attention or express surprise before becoming a warm way to greet someone.

1830s — 1870s

A voice across distance.

“Hello” became especially popular when the telephone arrived. It was recommended as an opening greeting because it was clear, short, and easy to hear over an early phone line.
