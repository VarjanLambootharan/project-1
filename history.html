One tiny word, countless beginnings

How hello
found its voice.

Before it opened conversations, “hello” was a shout across a room: a lively “hallo!” for surprise, attention, or a friend just spotted in the crowd. In the early 1800s, that quick call began its transformation into the welcome we know today.

1830s — 1870s

The word that crossed the wire.

Then came the telephone—and a new kind of distance. “Hello” was short, crisp, and easy to catch through the crackle of early phone lines, turning a call for attention into the first spark of connection.
